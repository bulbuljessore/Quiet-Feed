# Privacy Policy – Quiet Feed

Last updated: 2026-11-13

**Summary:** the extension does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension stores
- The keywords, YouTube channel names and settings that you type into the extension.
- A per-site count of hidden items, used only to show you a number in the popup.

This data is saved with the browser's extension storage on your device. If you use browser sync, your browser vendor may sync the keyword and channel lists between your own devices under that vendor's own privacy policy. We never receive this data.

## What the extension reads
To do its job, the extension reads the text of the pages you visit, YouTube page elements (channel names, titles, comments) and images on those pages. This happens locally in your browser to decide what to hide. Nothing is uploaded or sent to any server, including image content and recognized text.

## Network and third parties
The extension has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that contacts the internet. The text-recognition engine and its language data are bundled inside the extension and run on your device.

## Permissions
- Storage: save your lists and settings.
- Access to all websites: apply your filters on any site you visit and read images for on-device text recognition.
- Offscreen (Chromium browsers): run the on-device text recognition engine.

## Your control
You can edit or delete any keyword or channel in the popup at any time, turn filtering off, or remove the extension, which deletes its stored data.
