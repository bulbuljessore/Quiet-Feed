# Changelog

## 1.3.0
- YouTube: block or allow by words in a video title or channel name (not only by channel). "Show only" mode accepts allowed words such as education; blocked words and channels always win.
- YouTube presets: Kids 3-6 learning, Kids 7-12 education, block football, cartoons, games.
- Fixed: typing a topic (e.g. "education") into the allowed-channels list blanked YouTube; topics now go in the new words list.

## 1.2.0
- Adult content filter (optional): adult words, adult websites, YouTube names/titles, SafeSearch. "Show" button on hidden items, separate from the user's own filters.
- Blur mode for images and videos.
- YouTube channel blocker, only-show mode, related-content control, one-click add.
- Bengali / English popup.
- Update notice (popup banner and NEW badge), can be turned off.
- Removed all built-in names and keywords. Only user-added keywords are filtered.

## 1.0.0
- First release: keyword filter, text-in-image detection (OCR), Chromium and Firefox builds.
